# Cafe landing page

Create a polished, fully working Thai-first responsive cafe landing page. The fictional cafe is ชงช้า / CHONG CHA. Audience: people seeking a quiet coffee break. Its promise: กาแฟดี ไม่ต้องรีบ. Use only these fictional facts: open 08:00–17:00 daily; slow bar and espresso; Americano 80 baht, latte 95 baht, pour over 120 baht. Do not invent an address, testimonials, awards or sales claims. Mark the business information as demo content in the footer.

Use the shared photos coffee-01.jpg through coffee-04.jpg from demos/assets (frames from the same licensed footage). Deliver a complete static index.html with local relative assets, meaningful menu navigation, keyboard focus, mobile layout and reduced-motion support where applicable. No remote libraries or fonts. Desktop proof at 1440x1000; mobile at 390x844. Aim for the best finished result, not a wireframe.

Production budget: one initial implementation and one verification/repair pass; target 15 minutes, stop by 20 minutes. Record actual elapsed time and repair passes. You may inspect your own output. Do not inspect other conditions' outputs. You may use the existing tools and skills available to you. No external purchases. Save original source and short design decisions in your assigned output directory.

# ชงช้า — design decisions

Governing relationship: an unhurried reading rhythm leads; close-up coffee preparation supports it. The next meaningful action is to read the short coffee menu, with hours available directly from the navigation.

Palette: mist blue #e9eff0, paper white #fcfbf7, coffee-leaf green #293c35, quiet gray #586760, brass focus accent #a97822. Blue supplies breathing room without defaulting to a cream cafe aesthetic. The menu reverses to green so the actual decision receives a distinct visual destination.

Typography: local Leelawadee UI/Tahoma for Thai; Georgia for small English method labels and prices. Thai headline line-height 1.48 preserves tone-mark clearance. System fonts avoid external requests and layout shifts.

Signature: a small pause seal against the kettle image, paired with an offset second headline line. It expresses the cafe promise without ambient animation competing for attention. Repeated asymmetric photo corners provide a restrained vocabulary.

Source observations: coffee-01 shows the metallic kettle against a dark counter; coffee-02 shows the dry filter; coffee-04 shows brewing bubbles; coffee-03 shows steam obscuring the kettle. These are close-up preparation images, not evidence of seating or a location. Consequently the design presents moments of brewing and invents no venue interior, address, reviews or provenance claims.

Page is a one-shot marketing site. No application state, external services, order flow or application design contract is needed. Navigation uses native anchors, with a skip link, clear focus styles, 44px-plus main navigation targets through padded line-height, and reduced-motion behavior.

Verification and actual run timing are recorded in run.json. Desktop and mobile proof show the actual delivered HTML served under its final relative asset path.
